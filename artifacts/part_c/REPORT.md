# Part C: variant assembly and shop

## Implementation

- `scripts/ship.gd`: `full(accent, loadout = {})` retains the Kestrel root/interior, replaces individual modules from cached PackedScenes, tags their source family, and removes base gear when the selected keel supplies gear. `module`, `preview`, `prefab`, and recursive recolouring support real geometry throughout the shop and assembly. Exhaust resolves the selected propulsion modules' NozzleGlow positions.
- `scripts/planet_a.gd`: `_ship`, `_rivals`, `refresh_ship_loadout`, shop previews, carried modules and placement ghosts use selected prefabs. Loadout changes rebuild the ship and shelf miniatures before assembly; build FX operate on those selected nodes. Rivals use their seeded team loadouts. Desk requires twelve confirmed purchases. Existing build, outfit, erect, booster and launch camera behavior is retained.
- `scripts/ship_picker.gd`: modal picker with five colored family cards, prices, charge/refund differences, advantage/drawback descriptions, stat differences against the selected variant, and a rotating isolated SubViewport preview. A/D or arrows browse; E/Enter or BUY/SWAP confirms; Esc/Q or CLOSE returns to the shop. Unaffordable confirmations are disabled and failed keyboard purchases show a toast. Browsing never purchases a part.
- `scripts/main.gd`: opens the modal and freezes simulation/movement while keeping mode `play`; releases/restores mouse capture. Manifest names, design stats, route family/set info and go/no-go turret/scan readings use the chosen design.
- `scripts/hud.gd`, `scripts/ui.gd`: persistent shop design panel with credits, full chosen-design cost, TWR, delta-v, cargo, power margin, damage multiplier (armour), turret, scan, handling, wet mass, family/design name, set bonuses and unbought slots. The modal repeats these figures. Shared wrapping/summary helpers.
- `scripts/flight.gd`, `scripts/planet_b.gd`: player and rival ships retain their loadouts. OrbitShip has no ShipModel.full or Kestrel prefab caller; its campaign interior remains independent of competitive Sim.

## UX

All twelve slots initially preview Kestrel without being purchased. Approach a pedestal and press E to inspect five alternatives. Confirming buys that slot; later confirmation swaps it with a charge or refund of the difference. Close the picker to walk to the next pedestal. Once all twelve are confirmed, E at the desk starts the automated build while retaining player movement/camera controls. Legacy `buy_part` and autotest purchase paths continue through the existing Part B API.

## Picker defect follow-up (2026-11-17)

The reported stale Unbought line could not be reproduced in this checkout. Inspection shows `set_variant()` already mutates the live part dictionary's `purchased` flag, and picker `buy()` already refreshed both summaries. There is no separate checklist confirmation field. A missing purchase flag is therefore ruled out for the supplied implementation; the exact live-playthrough trigger remains unverified. The gameplay process skips ordinary HUD refresh while a modal is open, so refresh timing is now hardened: successful purchases/swaps emit `shop_changed`, the shop HUD subscribes, and picker purchases refresh the full HUD immediately.

`Sim.slot_confirmed()` now defines confirmation using the existing purchased flag plus a valid catalog family. Checklist/counts, Unbought, all_parts_bought, desk gate, reserved credits and auto-assembly consume that predicate. No second confirmation flag was introduced. Tests buy all twelve slots through the picker with mixed families, reject the desk at each incomplete step, check modal and HUD summaries immediately, assert Unbought is none and all_parts_bought is true, then start and finish the build. Charge, refund, preserved confirmation, failed purchase and external swap notification are covered.

Preview uses a viewport-scaled 260px minimum height (306px at 1600×900), AABB framing that accounts for rotation and aspect ratio, neutral grey environment with ambient illumination, key and fill lights, and slow rotation. Cards share their row height and use expanding spacers to align preview buttons; unaffordable variants remain inspectable by mouse and keyboard. Confirm remains disabled when unaffordable. Retained preview buttons for mouse access.

All nine tests/*.gd scripts and both requested tools exit 0 on Godot 4.7.2; individual logs are in this directory. Rendered capture tool also exits 0.
